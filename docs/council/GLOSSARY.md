# Council Review

Evaluation of the design considerations embodied in an implemented change.

## Language

**Council review**:
A review of an implemented change that evaluates whether its design choices and requested behavior make proportionate tradeoffs among the qualities required in the system's context, beyond implementation correctness.

**Council agenda**:
The consequential design choices, tradeoffs, and unresolved assumptions selected for council discussion.

**Council perspective**:
An assigned emphasis on particular quality attributes and their countervailing costs when evaluating a design choice. It directs scrutiny without establishing the system's requirements, ranking their importance, or prescribing a conclusion.
_Avoid_: Reviewer priority, lens

**Council reviewer**:
A participant who evaluates design choices through a council perspective and contributes evidence, objections, replies, and revisions to the assessment.

**Council moderator**:
The role responsible for focusing deliberation, surfacing unanswered objections, and bringing consequential choices to the user. Facilitating the discussion does not grant authority to accept a design decision.

**Council summary**:
A concise account of the central tradeoffs, strongest arguments, decisive evidence, and meaningful remaining dissent, preceding the council's recommendations.

**Council readout**:
The moderator's presentation of the council summary, recommendations, and necessary clarification or decision questions after internal deliberation.

**Suspended agenda item**:
An agenda item whose evaluation cannot meaningfully advance until identified missing information becomes available. Suspension expresses a limit on the assessment, not agreement or rejection.
